about my school

managment

Mohamed Hirsi Nur (Siidii) school works with Tayo for Education, and through them, it receives support from international partners like Emerge Global. These partners help the school by: Supporting vulnerable/low-income students Paying teacher salaries Improving the school’s learning environment Rehabilitating classrooms Providing learning materials Training teachers to improve quality of teaching This makes the school one of the stronger community-supported schools in Wadajir.

students community role

The school: Serves hundreds of students from Wadajir and nearby neighborhoods Plays a big role in giving education access to children from low-income families Is known for being inclusive and community-focused Helps many young boys and girls continue their education in a stable environment Because it offers both primary and secondary education,
families like the school since children do not need to transfer
elsewhere until they finish grade 12.

teachers

Teachers are a mix of experienced and young educators. Many of them receive training programs, which is uncommon in many Somali schools. The school emphasizes qualified teachers, especially in English, Math, and Science. This improves overall student performance.
