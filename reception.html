reception

The reception is the first point of contact between the customer and the establishment.
It serves as the welcome center, information hub, and coordination point for all customer-related activities.
The reception plays a crucial role in creating a positive first impression and ensuring smooth service flow.


                                       

pupose of reception

                             

1, Welcome customers warmly and professionally
2, Provide information about services, menu, or reservations
3, Manage customer check-in or seating
4, Handle reservations and inquiries
5, Communicate with other departments (kitchen, dining, management)
6, Maintain customer records and organize queues
