about my school

general veiw

Mohamed Hirsi Nur (Siidii) is a Primary and Secondary School located in Wadajir District,
Mogadishu. It is known for providing education to a large number of students
from the surrounding community. The school is
considered one of the important education centers in the area,
especially because it offers full schooling from grade 1 up to the secondary level. The name of the school is believed to be in honor
of Mohamed Hirsi Nur (Siidii) — a respected Somali figure possibly
connected to the Somali Youth League (SYL), making the school historically meaningful.

academic level

The school offers two major levels: Primary Level (Dugsiga Hoose & Dhexe) Grades 1–8 Focuses on basic literacy, numeracy, Islamic studies,
Somali language, and general subjects. Secondary Level (Dugsiga Sare) Grades 9–12 Students prepare for the Somalia National Secondary Examination.

Subjects typically include:

Mathematics English Physics Chemistry Biology Geography History Islamic Studies Arabic Civic Education The school has earned recognition because some
students performed very well in the national exams,
ranking among the top in the country.
