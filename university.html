hormuud university

Hormuud University waa jaamacad gaaban (private, non-profit) oo la asaasay sanadkii 2010. (HU)
Waxaa ku taalla degmada Wadajir District, Muqdisho, gobolka Banaadir. (HU)
Jaamacaddan waxay ku diiwaan gashan tahay hay’adda qaabilsan tacliinta sare ee Soomaaliya: (HU)
Ministry of Culture and Higher Education of Somalia. (HU)
Inkastoo ay jaamacad cusub tahay, waxay leedahay iskaashi caalami ah iyo xubinnimo ururo jaamacadeed,(HU)
sida Association of African Universities (AAU) iyo International Association of Universities (IAU). (HU)

waxa ay ku takhsusto iyo waxay ay bixiyaan

Waxay bixisaa barnaamijyo bachelor iyo master ah oo dhinacyo kala duwan ka mid yihiin
injineernimada, teknoolojiyadda, culuumta bulshada, maamulka,iwm. 
Ujeedada jaamacadda: “becoming unique national centre in education for engineering and other scientific specialist.
Providing quality education…” SERDEC EDUCATION 
Waxay sidoo kale leedahay iskaashi ganacsi iyo cilmi baaris oo gudaha Soomaaliya ah, iyadoo kaashanaysa
shirkado iyo ururo si kor loogu qaado tayada iyo fursadaha waxbarasho.

talooyin hadii aad xiiseeneeso inaad ku biirto (HU)

Hubi barnaamijka aad rabto: ma tahay bachelor ama master? Waa muhiim in barnaamijku yahay 
mid aqoonsi leh. (HU waxaa uu leeyahay aqoonsi rasmi ah.)
Fiiri shuruudaha galitaanka: sida shahaadada dugsiga sare, imtixaanada gudaha ee jaamacada kaa qaadeeso 
Booqo jaamacadda xarunteeda ku taalo (bulxubeey) ama la xiriir xafiiska talo bixinta ee qeybta (banaadir)
si aad u hesho xog dheeraad ah ee ku saabsan jamacada (Hormuud University)
