Universities in Mogadishu (Hormuud, SIMAD, mogadishu, Banaadir, Jazeera)

hormuud university

1. Hormuud University (HU) Establishment: Founded in 2010. Type: Private, Non-profit institution.
Origin: Founded by senior members of the Hormuud Telecommunications Company.
Focus: It places a special emphasis on Research, Engineering, and Technology. Key Faculty of Engineering.
Faculty of Computer Science & IT. Faculty of Economics & Management Sciences. Faculty of Sharia &
Faculty of Geoscience & Environment. Academics: Offers Postgraduate programs (Master's/PhD).

simad university

2. SIMAD University (SU) Establishment: Founded in November 1999.
Meaning: An acronym for Somali Institute of Management and Administration Development. Type: Private, Non-profit university.
Motto: "The Center of Knowledge and Wisdom."
Key Faculties: A large university offering numerous programs including:
Management Sciences.
Computing. Social Sciences.
Economics.
Education.
Engineering.
Medicine & Health Sciences.
Academics: Offers both Undergraduate and Postgraduate levels.


jazira university

3. Jazeera University (JU) Establishment: Founded in February 2010.
Origin: Founded by a group of Somali scholars.
Goal: To provide quality education, encourage research, and produce leaders.
Location: Located in a central and important area within Mogadishu.
Key Faculties: It has several faculties, including:
Faculty of Medicine.
Faculty of Engineering.
Faculty of Management & Economics.
Faculty of IT and Computer Science.
Accreditation: Recognized by the Ministry of Education, Culture and Higher Education of Somalia (MOECHE).


banadir university

4. mogadishu University (MU)
Mogadishu University is one of the oldest, largest, and most influential
Private Universities in Somalia. History and Foundation Establishment: Founded in 1997, during a period of civil strife and the absence
of a central government. This makes it one of the first universities to resume higher
education in Somalia. Type: Private, Non-profit university.
Goal: To restore the tradition of higher education in the country and provide quality
education based on Islamic principles and the needs of the Somali society.
Faculties and Programs Mogadishu University offers diverse academic programs at the Bachelor's (Undergraduate),
Master's (Postgraduate), and short-course certificate levels.

banadir university

5. Benadir University (BU) Establishment: Founded in October 2002.
Motto: "Cultivating Human Talents."
Original Purpose: Originally established as a Medical School to train Somali physicians.
Key Faculties: It is a large university with many faculties, including:
Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences (a core focus).
Many other faculties (offering over 77 programs).
Academics: Offers both Undergraduate and Postgraduate (Master's & PhD) levels.
Accreditation: Recognized by the World Health Organization (WHO).
